Most retail dashboards tell you what happened. This one tells you **where to look next**.

### Key features

<details>
<summary><b>🤖 Enterprise AI Adoption & Productivity Analysis   Python + Regression</b></summary>

### The question
Does enterprise AI adoption actually move the needle on labour productivity   or is the relationship weaker than the narrative suggests?

### What I did
- Collected and cleaned **80 quarters (2006–2025)** of U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics productivity data
- Matched against enterprise AI adoption rate (%) across the same period
- Built an **OLS linear regression**: y = 0.7826x − 10.772, **R² = 0.78%**
- Isolated the **Q1 2020 outlier** (~20.5 units) as a COVID-era statistical anomaly   output-per-worker spiked due to workforce reduction, not genuine productivity gains   and excluded it from modelling
- Produced publication-ready visualisations showing AI adoption growing exponentially while productivity trends remain flat

### Finding
Despite 70%+ growth in enterprise AI adoption since 2019, labour productivity has not followed proportionally. The most likely explanations: **adoption friction, skill gaps, and measurement lag**   not technology limitations.

🔗 [View Repository →](https://github.com/midhulms/Enterprise-AI-Adoption-Productivity-Analysis)

</details>
